Everything durable in [ky](K Y) lives in one Durable Object, and its SQLite holds every table. Your browser holds nothing, and resyncs over a socket. The sandbox and the Kitesurf browser are Cloudflare bindings, always on. Memory is just a module inside the object.
Your Mac is reached two ways. The model streams through a tunnel to VibeProxy. Tasks go down the runner socket to Claude Code. Today, a reconnect sends hello before the outbox. The object reports interrupted, then the real result reports again. The fix flushes the outbox first, so each result lands once.
Next, any computer can become a node. Its hello carries a manifest of tools and models. A reminders call waits on the Mac, with a ninety second deadline. A second box serves its own model, over its own tunnel.
Automations ride the scheduler inside the same object. Each fire submits to the main chat with a fixed operation id. A duplicate is dropped, and the reply is your update.
